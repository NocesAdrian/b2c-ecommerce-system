About Despera Merchandise

A student-built storefront inspired by the anime Despera, made to explore its aesthetic through a curated collection of apparel, accessories, and everyday items.

The inspiration

About the anime

Despera is the anime inspiration behind this collection. The store draws on its distinctive signal-inspired mood to present fan-made merchandise concepts. This is an independent school project and is not an official Despera shop or affiliated with the anime's creators.
